NEXTPOP Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Overview

NEXTPOP is a mobile game published under the Storynest Creations developer account. NEXTPOP does not create user accounts and does not operate its own database of player profiles. Game progress and settings are stored locally on the user’s device. Third-party services used by the app, including Google AdMob and RevenueCat, may process technical, advertising, consent, and purchase-related information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Data stored on your device

NEXTPOP stores gameplay information such as scores, streaks, unlocked themes, settings, and local progress on your device. This information is not sent to a NEXTPOP player-profile database. You can remove local app data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app, subject to your device and Google Play settings.

Advertising and consent

NEXTPOP uses Google AdMob to show ads to non-premium users. Google may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices for advertising, fraud prevention, security, measurement, and related purposes. Where required, the app uses Google’s consent flow before requesting ads. Privacy choices can be revisited from Privacy Options in NEXTPOP Settings.

Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Google Play. NEXTPOP does not receive or store your payment card details. RevenueCat is used to validate purchases, restore purchases, and determine whether Premium is active. RevenueCat may process purchase tokens, product and transaction information, app/device information, and related technical data needed to provide those services.

Cookies and similar technologies

NEXTPOP does not use browser cookies as part of normal in-app gameplay. Third-party SDKs may use device identifiers, SDK storage, or similar technologies as described in their own privacy documentation.

Third-party services

Google Play, Google AdMob, and RevenueCat operate their own systems and may process data on servers outside your country. Their processing, retention, security, and international-transfer practices are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to personal data, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent. Because NEXTPOP does not maintain its own player-profile database, some requests concerning data held by Google or RevenueCat may need to be directed to those providers. You can still contact us with any privacy question about NEXTPOP.
